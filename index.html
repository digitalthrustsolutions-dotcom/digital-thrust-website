WHY PROPTECH MATTERS

Smarter technology.
Stronger brokerages.

Most brokerages lose money after the lead arrives, not before. Enquiries sit on agents’ personal phones, nobody can see whether they were answered, and when an agent leaves the client list leaves too. The fix is owning the route from advert to closed deal.

⌕Every lead is attributedYou know which listing and which campaign produced it.
◇The database is yoursContacts and conversations stay with the business, not the agent.
